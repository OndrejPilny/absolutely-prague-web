Your Tours: Beyond Prague

Beyond Prague's cobblestoned streets lies the heart of Bohemia—medieval towns, spa retreats, UNESCO World Heritage sites, and landscapes that have shaped Czech history for centuries. Here are the most popular day trips from Prague, but if you have another destination in mind, just let me know.

How It Works

All day trips include comfortable, air-conditioned transportation with a professional driver, along with me as your guide throughout the journey. We'll handle all the logistics—you simply enjoy the experience.

Every trip can be enhanced with exclusive visits, additional stops, or detours that match your interests. And if you're traveling onward to Vienna, Munich, or Berlin, some of these destinations can be visited en route, making the most of your journey through Central Europe.

Kutna Hora

6-9 hours

UNESCO town shaped by medieval silver wealth, with striking Gothic architecture.

Highlights: St. Barbara's Cathedral, Italian Court, Sedlec Ossuary

Terezin

5-7 hours

A meaningful historical journey through the memorial, fortress, and ghetto museum.

Highlights: Terezin Memorial, Small Fortress, Holocaust history

Cesky Krumlov

9-12 hours

Fairy-tale UNESCO destination with dramatic castle views and winding river streets.

Highlights: Castle complex, medieval center, Vltava panoramas

Karlovy Vary

7-9 hours

Elegant spa atmosphere, colonnades, and mineral springs in a Belle Epoque setting.

Highlights: Historic spa town, hot springs, refined architecture

Plzen (Pilsen)

7-9 hours

Beer heritage, Gothic landmarks, and compelling WWII liberation history.

Highlights: Pilsner Urquell Brewery, cathedral, US Army liberation context

Karlstejn Castle

5 hours

14th-century hilltop fortress founded by Charles IV in scenic Bohemian countryside.

Highlights: Gothic castle, royal history, valley viewpoints

Traveling Onward? Let's Make the Journey Part of the Experience

If you're continuing to other Central European destinations, several of these trips work beautifully as stops along the way:

  • To Vienna – Český Krumlov en route
  • To Munich – Plzeň en route
  • To Berlin – Terezín en route

Instead of a simple transfer, we'll turn your travel day into an experience, exploring one of these remarkable places before you continue on to your next destination.
