About Jana Pilna - Founder and Lead Guide

Hello, I am Jana Pilna (nee Markova), the founder and head guide of Absolutely Prague, and I have been guiding travelers through my home city since 2014. But my connection to Prague and to the art of storytelling goes back much further than that.

I was literally born to do this.

Not just because I grew up wandering the cobblestoned streets of Prague's Old Town, or because my mother was a tour guide before me, making me the third generation in my family to fall in love with sharing this city, a tradition that started with my great-aunt and my godmother Leopoldina. But because I was actually born on February 21st, International Tour Guide Day.

Some people might call that coincidence. I call it destiny.

As a little girl, I would tag along on my mom's tours, watching her bring Prague's legends to life for travelers from around the world. I did not know it then, but those cobblestoned streets and their stories were shaping my future. By the time I officially became a licensed guide in 2014, it felt natural.

Over the years, what started as a job became a passion. I earned two master's degrees, one in Tourism from the University of Economics in Prague (with a semester at the University of Northern Colorado), and another in Theater Studies from Charles University. I represented the Czech Republic at the New York Times Travel Show during my internship with Czech Tourism in New York. I have guided everyone from individual travelers to university groups, from Venice Simplon-Orient-Express guests to Pittsburgh and NYU students on study tours.

When tourism stopped during COVID, it crystallized something I had always known: guiding is not just what I do. It is who I am. In a world of apps and audio guides, I offer something technology cannot, a human connection, stories told with passion, and the kind of insider access that comes from a lifetime of loving this city.

What Makes My Tours Different

I am not just licensed to guide Prague and the Czech Republic. I am also an official guide of the Jewish Museum in Prague, which requires a specialized exam in Jewish traditions, religion, and Czech Jewish history. Beyond that, I have built long-term partnerships as a trusted guide at some of Prague's most prestigious cultural institutions: the Czech National Theatre, the Estates Theatre, the State Opera, and the Municipal House.

I have traveled to the US, Canada, Israel, China, Australia, Fiji, Egypt, the Maldives, and across Europe, so I understand what you need when you are exploring a new city. I know what it is like to be a traveler. And I know what it feels like to come home to the place you love most.

When I am Not Guiding

You will find me at the theater, hiking through the beautiful Czech countryside, or exploring Prague's newest cafes and restaurants with my husband. I have a notoriously sweet tooth, so we are always on the hunt for the best desserts and coffee. At home, I love to cook and bake, read, and preserve seasonal fruits and vegetables. And of course, there is plenty of time devoted to spoiling our beloved pets.

Let's Explore Together

Whether you are here for a day or a week, whether you want to dive deep into history and culture or explore as much as possible, taste authentic Czech cuisine or have hands-on experiences discovering local crafts, I would love to show you my Prague. The one that shaped me. The one I am still discovering. The one that is waiting for you.
