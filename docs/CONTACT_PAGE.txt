TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FORM HANDLING:

Option A: Email Form (simplest)
- Form submits via PHP/JavaScript to email
- Auto-reply confirmation email to user
- Copy to jana.pilna.prague@gmail.com

Option B: Form Service (recommended)
- Use Formspree, Netlify Forms, or similar
- Stores submissions + emails you
- Easier spam protection
- Can integrate with CRM later

Option C: Custom Backend
- Store in database
- More control, but more complex

REQUIRED FIELDS:
- Name, Email (must validate format)
- All other fields optional

SPAM PROTECTION:
- Add reCAPTCHA or hCaptcha (invisible version)
- Honeypot field (hidden from users)

EMAIL NOTIFICATIONS:
When form submitted, send:

1. Auto-reply to user:
   Subject: "Thanks for contacting Absolutely Prague"
   Body: "Hi [Name], Thanks for reaching out! I've received your inquiry 
   and will respond within 24 hours. In the meantime, feel free to explore 
   [link to tours]. - Jana"
