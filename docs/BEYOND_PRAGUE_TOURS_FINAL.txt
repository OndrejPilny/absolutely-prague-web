DAY TRIP 1
-------------------

**Kutná Hora**
*6 hours*

The lovely town of Kutná Hora is waiting for your visit. On this tour, I'll 
take you back to medieval times when this town was one of the wealthiest in 
the Bohemian Kingdom, thanks to its silver mines. You'll enjoy the beauty of 
the Czech landscape on the way in a comfortable car and learn about Czech 
history while exploring this UNESCO World Heritage site. We'll visit the 
stunning Gothic St. Barbara's Cathedral, the Italian Court (former royal 
mint), and the famous Sedlec Ossuary—a chapel decorated with the bones of 
over 40,000 people.

**Highlights:** UNESCO World Heritage, St. Barbara's Cathedral, Sedlec Ossuary, 
medieval silver mining history


-------------------
DAY TRIP 2
-------------------

**Terezín**
*5 hours*

Terezín Memorial is located in a former garrison town that was transformed 
into a Jewish ghetto during Nazi occupation. This tour will take you to the 
concentration camp where more than 150,000 people passed through between 1941 
and 1945. We'll visit the Small Fortress, the Ghetto Museum, and the Memorial, 
learning about both the horrific conditions and the remarkable resilience of 
those imprisoned here. This is a deeply moving experience that honors the 
memory of those who suffered and perished during the Holocaust.

**Highlights:** Terezín Memorial, Small Fortress, Ghetto Museum, Holocaust history


-------------------
DAY TRIP 3
-------------------

**Český Krumlov**
*9 hours*

This picturesque town in the South Bohemian Region is one of the most beautiful 
in all of Europe. You'll be rewarded with an unforgettable day full of stories, 
stunning views, and charming local restaurants and shops. We'll explore the 
magnificent castle complex (second largest in the Czech Republic after Prague 
Castle), wander the winding medieval streets, and enjoy the town's fairy-tale 
atmosphere on the banks of the Vltava River. This UNESCO World Heritage site 
feels like stepping back in time—without the crowds you'd find in more famous 
European destinations.

**Highlights:** UNESCO World Heritage, Český Krumlov Castle, medieval old town, 
Vltava River views


-------------------
DAY TRIP 4
-------------------

**Karlovy Vary**
*7 hours*

This tour will give you a chance to experience a holiday as it was enjoyed in 
the 19th century. Let's stroll around the well-known spa town of Karlovy Vary, 
also known as Carlsbad in English. You'll be surprised that the relaxed 
atmosphere of this healing place remains in all corners of this charming town. 
We'll walk along the colonnades, sample the hot mineral springs (yes, you can 
drink them!), and explore the elegant architecture that has attracted European 
aristocracy and celebrities for centuries. The drive through the West Bohemian 
landscape is beautiful in itself.

**Highlights:** Historic spa town, hot mineral springs, elegant colonnades, 
Belle Époque architecture


-------------------
DAY TRIP 5
-------------------

**Plzeň (Pilsen)**
*7 hours*

This day trip introduces you not only to the history of Plzeň but also 
highlights its significant role at the end of World War II and liberation 
by the US Army. On the journey to this lovely town in Western Bohemia, we'll 
see the Czech countryside and find the hop fields that provide the main 
ingredient for the famous Pilsner Urquell Brewery. We can visit the brewery 
itself (if you'd like), explore the historic city center with its beautiful 
Gothic cathedral, and learn about the town's industrial heritage and its 
special connection to American history.

**Highlights:** Pilsner Urquell Brewery, WWII liberation history, Gothic 
cathedral, hop fields


-------------------
DAY TRIP 6
-------------------

**Karlštejn Castle**
*5 hours*

Karlštejn Castle rises dramatically above the Berounka River valley, just 
30 kilometers southwest of Prague. Built in the 14th century by Charles IV, 
King of Bohemia and Holy Roman Emperor, this Gothic fortress was designed to 
safeguard royal treasures and the Crown Jewels of the Holy Roman Empire. 
We'll climb up to the castle, explore its impressive fortifications, and hear 
stories of medieval power, intrigue, and the legendary Chapel of the Holy Cross 
with its precious stone decoration. The surrounding countryside is perfect for 
a peaceful walk before or after the castle visit.

**Highlights:** 14th-century Gothic castle, dramatic hilltop setting, royal 
history, scenic Bohemian countryside
