ČÁST 2: ABOUT PAGE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[NADPIS:]
About Jana Pilná – Founder & Lead Guide


[HLAVNÍ TEXT:]

Hello, I am Jana Pilná (neé Marková), the founder and head guide of Absolutely 
Prague, and I've been guiding travelers through my home city since 2014. But my 
connection to Prague—and to the art of storytelling—goes back much further than that.

I was literally born to do this.

Not just because I grew up wandering the cobblestoned streets of Prague's Old Town, 
or because my mother was a tour guide before me, making me the third generation in 
my family to fall in love with sharing this city—a tradition that started with my 
great-aunt and my godmother Leopoldina. But because I was actually born on 
February 21st—International Tour Guide Day.

Some people might call that coincidence. I call it destiny.

As a little girl, I'd tag along on my mom's tours, watching her bring Prague's 
legends to life for travelers from around the world. I didn't know it then, but 
those cobblestoned streets and their stories were shaping my future. By the time 
I officially became a licensed guide in 2014, it felt natural.

Over the years, what started as a job became a passion. I earned two master's 
degrees—one in Tourism from the University of Economics in Prague (with a semester 
at the University of Northern Colorado), and another in Theater Studies from 
Charles University, one of the world's oldest universities. I've represented the 
Czech Republic at the New York Times Travel Show during my internship with Czech 
Tourism in New York. I've guided everyone from individual travelers to university 
groups, from Venice Simplon-Orient-Express guests to Pittsburgh and NYU students 
on study tours.

When tourism stopped during COVID, it crystallized something I'd always known: 
guiding isn't just what I do. It's who I am. In a world of apps and audio guides, 
I offer something technology can't—a human connection, stories told with passion, 
and the kind of insider access that comes from a lifetime of loving this city.


**What Makes My Tours Different**

I'm not just licensed to guide Prague and the Czech Republic. I'm also an official 
guide of the Jewish Museum in Prague, which requires a specialized exam in Jewish 
traditions, religion, and Czech Jewish history. Beyond that, I've built long-term 
partnerships as a trusted guide at some of Prague's most prestigious cultural 
institutions—the Czech National Theatre, the Estates Theatre, the State Opera, 
and the Municipal House. These places aren't just on my itinerary; they're woven 
into my life, and I know their stories intimately.

I've traveled to the US, Canada, Israel, China, Australia, Fiji, Egypt, the 
Maldives, and across Europe—so I understand what you need when you're exploring 
a new city. I know what it's like to be a traveler. And I know what it feels like 
to come home to the place you love most.


**When I'm Not Guiding**

You'll find me at the theater (my favorite escape), hiking through the beautiful 
Czech countryside, or exploring Prague's newest cafés and restaurants with my 
husband—I have a notoriously sweet tooth, so we're always on the hunt for the 
best desserts and coffee. At home, I love to cook and bake, read, and preserve 
seasonal fruits and vegetables. And of course, there's plenty of time devoted to 
spoiling our beloved pets.


**Let's Explore Together**

Whether you're here for a day or a week, whether you want to dive deep into history 
and culture or explore as much as possible, taste authentic Czech cuisine or have 
hands-on experiences discovering local crafts—I'd love to show you my Prague. 
The one that shaped me. The one I'm still discovering. The one that's waiting for you.

BRAND VOICE REMINDERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

TONE: "Luxury Friend" not corporate
- Warm but refined
- Personal but professional
- Story-driven, not sales-driven
- Specific examples over generic superlatives

AVOID:
- "Curated" "bespoke" (too corporate)
- "What I learned" (cliché)
- Third person (always first person)
- Overpromising ("once in a lifetime")

EMBRACE:
- "My Prague" "together" "with me"
- Personal stories (Leopoldina, February 21st)
- Specific details (sweet tooth, theater lover)
- Collaborative language
