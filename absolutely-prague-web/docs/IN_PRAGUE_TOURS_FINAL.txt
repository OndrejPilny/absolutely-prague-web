[INTRO TEXT:]

At Absolutely Prague, every tour is designed around how you like to travel. 
You choose how long to explore, how to get around, and what captures your 
interest most. The tours below are here to inspire you—but I'm always happy 
to create something completely unique based on what excites you.

**How It Works**

Tours are typically offered as half-day (4 hours) or full-day (8 hours) 
experiences, starting at whatever time works best for you. I'll meet you 
in the lobby of your hotel, and we can finish either back at your hotel or 
at a destination of your choice in the city.

We can explore Prague on foot (taking advantage of the tram system when 
needed), or I can arrange a private car with one of the transportation 
companies I work with—all offering the highest standards of service.


**My Philosophy: Every Great Prague Tour Should Include These Three Elements**

Based on over 10 years of guiding, I've learned that the most memorable 
tours combine:

• **A Tower** – Prague is more than its historical center. Seeing it from 
  above gives you perspective on how the city flows together. (Don't worry—
  some towers have elevators!)

• **An Interior** – Whether it's a Baroque library, Gothic cathedral, or 
  19th-century theatre, stepping inside brings history to life in ways the 
  streets alone can't.

• **Wandering the Streets** – This is where stories live. Where you feel 
  the city's rhythm. Where we can pause for coffee, stumble upon hidden 
  courtyards, and let Prague surprise you.


**Create Your Own**

The tours listed below are suggestions to give you a sense of what's possible. 
But if you have something specific in mind—a passion for Art Nouveau architecture, 
a desire to explore Prague's theatre scene, or anything else that excites you—
just ask. I love designing custom itineraries that match your interests perfectly.

You can also mix and match tours throughout your stay to experience as much 
of Prague as possible!

TOUR 1
-------------------

**Prague Intro Tour**
*Half-Day (4 hours)*

Perfect for first-time visitors or anyone with limited time. I'll show you 
Prague's most famous landmarks—the Astronomical Clock, Charles Bridge, and 
Prague Castle—while weaving in Czech history that brings the buildings to 
life. Along the way, I'll share recommendations for where to eat, shop, and 
explore on your own.

**Highlights:** Astronomical Clock, Charles Bridge, Prague Castle, local tips


-------------------
TOUR 2
-------------------

**Prague Day Tour**
*Full-Day (8 hours)*

If you have a full day, let me show you why Prague is called the City of a 
Hundred Spires. This immersive tour covers the main highlights—Municipal House, 
Old Town Square, Wenceslas Square, Estates Theatre, Charles Bridge, John Lennon 
Wall, and Prague Castle—while I share stories that span centuries of Czech 
history. You'll leave with a deep understanding of the city and plenty of ideas 
for your remaining time here.

**Highlights:** All major landmarks, comprehensive history, insider recommendations


-------------------
TOUR 3
-------------------

**Jewish Quarter**
*Half-Day (4 hours) or Full-Day*

Prague's Jewish Quarter tells centuries of history—both tragic and resilient. 
During this tour, we'll visit the entire Jewish Museum in Prague, including 
the Maisel Synagogue, Pinkas Synagogue, Chevra Kadisha, Klausen Synagogue, 
Spanish Synagogue, Old-New Synagogue, and the Old Jewish Cemetery. Some 
synagogues are still used for worship today; others function as museums 
displaying religious objects. You'll hear stories from ancient times and 
recent history that bring this community's legacy to life. This tour can 
be extended to a full-day Jewish Prague experience exploring the broader 
Jewish legacy in the city.

**Highlights:** Jewish Museum complex, Old-New Synagogue, Old Jewish Cemetery


-------------------
TOUR 4
-------------------

**Tasty Prague – Foodie Tour**
*Half-Day or Full-Day*

Prague is a city full of aromas and flavors! Let me introduce you to Czech 
cuisine at its best. We'll visit a local bakery, a delicatessen, a restaurant, 
and a confectionery. You'll try Czech pastries and starters, sample the most 
traditional meals, and satisfy your sweet tooth with Czech desserts. Of course, 
we won't miss the chance to taste Czech wine and beer! I can design this as 
a walking tour in one neighborhood, or as a driving tour that explores multiple 
districts and gives you a broader sense of the city's culinary landscape.

**Highlights:** Czech pastries, traditional meals, local wine and beer, authentic eateries


-------------------
TOUR 5
-------------------

**Prague Castle District**
*Half-Day or Full-Day (8 hours)*

The Prague Castle complex sits on a hilltop above the city and is one of the 
largest ancient castles in the world. We can start with the Strahov Monastery 
Library to see stunning Baroque library halls, or visit Loreto, a monastery 
famous for its religious collection and pilgrimage site. Inside the Castle 
complex, you can choose to enter St. Vitus Cathedral and the Royal Palace. 
We'll finish at Lobkowicz Palace, the only privately owned property in the 
Castle grounds, with an extraordinary collection of art and artifacts.

**Highlights:** Prague Castle, Strahov Library, St. Vitus Cathedral, Lobkowicz Palace


-------------------
TOUR 6
-------------------

**Lesser Town (Malá Strana)**
*Half-Day or Full-Day*

Malá Strana is Prague's most romantic neighborhood—a district of ornate Baroque 
palaces, beautiful gardens, and winding streets. We'll cross the Charles Bridge, 
walk through Kampa Park, and stop at the John Lennon Wall. You'll visit the 
Church of the Infant Jesus of Prague to see his famous collection of dresses, 
and St. Nicolas Church, the most beautiful Baroque church north of the Alps. 
We'll wander Nerudova Street with its unusual house markers, and in summer, 
explore one of the gardens (Wallenstein, Vrtba, or Palace Gardens).

**Highlights:** Charles Bridge, John Lennon Wall, St. Nicolas Church, gardens


-------------------
TOUR 7
-------------------

**Vyšehrad & Surroundings**
*Half-Day (4 hours)*

Vyšehrad, which means "Upper Castle," is connected to some of the oldest Czech 
legends and offers a peaceful escape from tourist crowds. We'll visit the 
Neo-Gothic Basilica of Saints Peter and Paul, walk through the National Cemetery 
where Czech greats are buried, and enjoy panoramic views over the Vltava River. 
On our way back, we'll stop to admire Cubist-style houses and visit Emmaus 
Monastery, where Gothic meets Baroque.

**Highlights:** Vyšehrad fortress, Neo-Gothic basilica, panoramic views, Cubist architecture


-------------------
TOUR 8
-------------------

**Vinohrady & Žižkov Neighborhoods**
*Half-Day (4 hours)*

Let's leave the tourist center and explore two of Prague's most authentic 
neighborhoods. Vinohrady is a charming residential area filled with Art Nouveau 
buildings, cozy cafés, and boutique shops. Žižkov preserves its bohemian spirit 
as a former working-class district. We'll visit Peace Square, take an elevator 
to the observation deck of Žižkov TV Tower, and finish at the hilltop Vítkov 
Memorial with incredible views.

**Highlights:** Residential Prague, Art Nouveau architecture, TV Tower, local atmosphere


-------------------
TOUR 9
-------------------

**Holešovice & Letná Neighborhoods**
*Half-Day (4 hours)*

This tour takes you to Prague's trendiest neighborhoods, where former industrial 
districts have transformed into hubs of design, art, and culture. We'll walk 
through Letná Park to see the giant Metronome, explore Holešovice's galleries 
and design shops, and visit the Czech National Gallery's Trade Fair Palace. 
We can end at the Prague Exhibition Grounds, built in 1891 and connected to 
the massive Stromovka Park.

**Highlights:** Letná Park, Metronome, National Gallery, contemporary art scene


-------------------
TOUR 10
-------------------

**Prague for Families**
*Flexible Duration*

Traveling with kids? I'm happy to adjust any itinerary to keep younger travelers 
engaged and entertained. I can also arrange special interactive activities—
workshops, hands-on experiences, or fun rides—that educate and delight children 
at the same time. Just let me know their ages and interests, and I'll create 
a tour the whole family will enjoy.

**Highlights:** Kid-friendly pacing, interactive activities, educational fun
