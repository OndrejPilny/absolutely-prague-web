Experiences

Your Tours: In Prague

Every tour is designed around how you like to travel. Choose duration, style, and pace. The ideas below are inspiration, and I am always happy to create something completely unique for you.

How It Works

Tours are typically offered as half-day (4 hours) or full-day (8 hours) experiences, starting at whatever time works best for you. I will meet you in your hotel lobby, and we can finish either back at your hotel or at another destination in the city.

We can explore on foot (using Prague's tram system when useful), or I can arrange a private car with trusted transportation partners.

My Philosophy: Three Essential Elements

  • A Tower: See Prague from above and understand how the city flows together.
  • An Interior: Step inside places where history and culture truly come alive.
  • Wandering the Streets: Find stories, hidden courtyards, and local rhythm.

Create Your Own

You can also mix and match tours throughout your stay. If you have a specific passion, from Art Nouveau architecture to Prague's theatre scene, I will design a custom itinerary around it.

Suggested private tours in Prague

Prague Intro Tour

Half-Day (4 hours)

Perfect for first-time visitors, featuring major landmarks and practical local tips.

Highlights: Astronomical Clock, Charles Bridge, Prague Castle

Prague Day Tour

Full-Day (8 hours)

A full immersion in Prague's history, architecture, and neighborhoods.

Highlights: Municipal House, Old Town Square, Prague Castle, John Lennon Wall

Jewish Quarter

Half-Day (4 hours) or Full-Day

Explore the Jewish Museum complex with historical context, religious traditions, and human stories.

Highlights: Old-New Synagogue, Spanish Synagogue, Old Jewish Cemetery

Tasty Prague - Foodie Tour

Half-Day or Full-Day

Discover Czech flavors through bakeries, restaurants, sweets, wine, and beer tastings.

Highlights: Pastries, traditional meals, desserts, local wine and beer

Prague Castle District

Half-Day or Full-Day

Hilltop history, monastic treasures, cathedral interiors, and exceptional collections.

Highlights: Strahov Library, St. Vitus Cathedral, Lobkowicz Palace

Lesser Town (Mala Strana)

Half-Day or Full-Day

Romantic Baroque district with gardens, churches, and hidden lanes near Charles Bridge.

Highlights: Kampa, John Lennon Wall, St. Nicolas Church, Nerudova Street

Vysehrad and Surroundings

Half-Day (4 hours)

A quieter side of Prague with legends, basilica architecture, and broad river views.

Highlights: Vysehrad fortress, National Cemetery, Emmaus Monastery

Vinohrady and Zizkov Neighborhoods

Half-Day (4 hours)

Authentic residential Prague with Art Nouveau details and local atmosphere.

Highlights: Peace Square, Zizkov TV Tower, Vitkov Memorial

Holesovice and Letna Neighborhoods

Half-Day (4 hours)

Design-forward districts where industrial heritage meets contemporary art and culture.

Highlights: Letna Park, Metronome, Trade Fair Palace

Prague for Families

Flexible Duration

Customized pacing and interactive elements to keep children curious and engaged.

Highlights: Family-friendly planning, workshops, educational fun
