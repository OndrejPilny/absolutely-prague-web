Your Tours: In Prague

At Absolutely Prague, every tour is designed around how you like to travel. You choose how long to explore, how to get around, and what captures your interest most. The tours below are here to inspire you—but I'm always happy to create something completely unique based on what excites you.

How It Works

Tours are typically offered as half-day (4 hours) or full-day (8 hours) experiences, starting at whatever time works best for you. I'll meet you in the lobby of your hotel, and we can finish either back at your hotel or at a destination of your choice in the city.

We can explore Prague on foot (taking advantage of the tram system when needed), or I can arrange a private car with one of the transportation companies I work with—all offering the highest standards of service.

My Philosophy: Every Great Prague Tour Should Include These Three Elements

Based on over 10 years of guiding, I've learned that the most memorable tours combine:

  • A Tower – Prague is more than its historical center. Seeing it from above gives you perspective on how the city flows together. (Don't worry—some towers have elevators!)
  • An Interior – Whether it's a Baroque library, Gothic cathedral, or 19th-century theatre, stepping inside brings history to life in ways the streets alone can't.
  • Wandering the Streets – This is where stories live. Where you feel the city's rhythm. Where we can pause for coffee, stumble upon hidden courtyards, and let Prague surprise you.

Create Your Own

The tours listed below are suggestions to give you a sense of what's possible. But if you have something specific in mind—a passion for Art Nouveau architecture, a desire to explore Prague's theatre scene, or anything else that excites you—just ask. I love designing custom itineraries that match your interests perfectly.

You can also mix and match tours throughout your stay to experience as much of Prague as possible!

Jewish Quarter

Half-Day (4 hours)

Explore the Jewish Museum complex with historical context, religious traditions, and human stories.

Highlights: Old-New Synagogue, Spanish Synagogue, Old Jewish Cemetery

Tasty Prague - Foodie Tour

Half-Day (4 hours)

Discover Czech flavors through bakeries, restaurants, sweets, wine, and beer tastings.

Highlights: Pastries, traditional meals, desserts, local wine and beer

Prague Castle District

Half-Day or Full-Day

Hilltop history, monastic treasures, cathedral interiors, and exceptional collections.

Highlights: Strahov Library, St. Vitus Cathedral, Lobkowicz Palace

Old Town Tour

Half-Day (4 hours)

Prague's historic core, from the Astronomical Clock to quiet courtyards and hidden memorials.

Highlights: Astronomical Clock, Old Town Square, Church of Our Lady before Tyn, Municipal House

New Town Tour

Half-Day (4 hours)

Wenceslas Square, Art Nouveau passages, and modern landmarks in Prague's 19th-century heart.

Highlights: Wenceslas Square, National Museum, Lucerna Palace, Dancing House

Lesser Town (Mala Strana)

Half-Day (4 hours)

Romantic Baroque district with gardens, churches, and hidden lanes near Charles Bridge.

Highlights: Charles Bridge, John Lennon Wall, St. Nicolas Church, gardens

Vysehrad and Surroundings

Half-Day (4 hours)

A quieter side of Prague with legends, basilica architecture, and broad river views.

Highlights: Vysehrad fortress, National Cemetery, Emmaus Monastery

Vinohrady and Zizkov Neighborhoods

Holesovice and Letna Neighborhoods

Half-Day (4 hours)

Highlights: Letna Park, Metronome, Trade Fair Palace
